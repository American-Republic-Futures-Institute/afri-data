It **does not** grant any rights to original government files, third-party geographic materials, research institute publications, trademarks or datasets not owned by contributors.

The initial source files originate from the U.S. Census Bureau. Most U.S. federal government works are public domain under 17 USC 105, subject to source-specific exceptions or third-party material. The public registry includes nonprofits and academic organizations whose datasets have *not* been authorized for redistribution.

Before publishing an independent dataset, record its exact license or access agreement, permitted uses, attribution and derivative/redistribution constraints. Do not equate a public webpage or API response with permissive republishing. When uncertain, link to the source and do not copy or redistribute.

Source provenance and official links should be displayed even when redistribution is permitted. ARFI does not claim sponsorship or endorsement by the U.S. Census Bureau or any other data publisher.
